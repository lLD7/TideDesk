# TideDesk 图标

设计：深石墨色圆角底，青绿色网格组成希腊字母 Γ，玫红色格子提示正负敞口。与原生深色金融终端的热力图配色一致。

- `TideDesk-1024.png`：透明背景的 1024px 主图。
- `TideDesk.iconset/`：macOS 16–1024px 图标尺寸。
- `TideDesk.icns`：应用使用的图标。

制作方式：内置 image_gen 工具生成原图；macOS sips 仅转换尺寸，iconutil 生成 ICNS。

生成提示词：

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final production macOS app icon for GammaGrid, an options exposure and heatmap desktop terminal. Create one square 1024x1024 icon on real transparent background, a single dark graphite rounded square tile occupying about 86 percent of canvas, straight-on flat orthographic view, centered, equal transparent margins. The tile has elegant subtle beveled edges, restrained satin material and deep charcoal #0D1118. In the center a very bold geometric Greek uppercase Gamma Γ built from a precise small heatmap grid of thick rounded rectangular cells: three cells horizontally across the top, three cells vertically down the left including the shared corner. Vibrant emerald/teal #00D6A5 on the main Gamma cells. To the lower right, two or three smaller subdued rose-magenta #EC477C cells complete a sparse grid motif, clearly separated from the green Gamma. Minimal sophisticated financial analytics identity, no stock arrow clichés. Strong readable silhouette at 32px, very clean high contrast, restrained depth and gentle highlight, no noisy textures or fine chart lines, no tiny detail. No letters or text besides the abstract Gamma geometry. No watermark, no mockup environment, no perspective, no extra icons, no external glow or cast shadow outside the rounded tile. Preserve true transparent alpha outside the tile.

TideDesk 沿用原 GammaGrid 图标设计，保留 Γ 与热力图网格的含义。
